# 05 — Mathematics for AI

**Stage:** Foundations (parallel with Python) · **Prerequisites:** 01-02; algebra refresh as needed · **Estimate:** 45-75 hours

## Outcomes

Interpret the mathematical objects behind data, models, uncertainty, loss, and optimization and translate small derivations into code.

## Planned lessons

Algebra and functions; vectors, matrices, products, norms, and eigenvectors; derivatives, chain rule, partial derivatives, gradients; probability and conditional probability; common distributions; expectation and variance; sampling and confidence; optimization and gradient descent; logarithms, entropy, and information.
