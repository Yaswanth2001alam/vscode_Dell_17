# 17 — Large Language Models

**Stage:** Advanced · **Prerequisites:** 16 · **Estimate:** 30-50 hours

## Outcomes

Explain how language models are tokenized, pretrained, prompted, sampled, evaluated, and deployed—and what their outputs do not guarantee.

## Planned lessons

Tokenizers and embeddings; transformer recap; next-token objectives; pretraining and data; inference and decoding; context windows; evaluation; hallucinations; alignment; privacy, bias, and limits.

**Practice milestone:** run a local or explicitly identified service-based example and evaluate a fixed prompt set with documented limits. **Status:** planned; model/API costs will be disclosed in lessons.
