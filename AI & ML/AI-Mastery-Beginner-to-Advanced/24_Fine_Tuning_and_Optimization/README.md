# 24 — Fine-tuning and Optimization

**Stage:** Advanced · **Prerequisites:** 14, 17 · **Estimate:** 25-45 hours

## Outcomes

Decide whether fine-tuning is appropriate and compare quality, data, compute, latency, and deployment tradeoffs.

## Planned lessons

Data curation; supervised fine-tuning; adapters/LoRA; preference optimization concepts; quantization; distillation; batching and caching; inference optimization; evaluation and rollback.

**Practice milestone:** compare a baseline with a small adaptation or quantization experiment; report costs and regressions. **Status:** planned; hardware/API requirements to be disclosed.
