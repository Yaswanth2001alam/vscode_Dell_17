# 34 — Distributed AI and Advanced Training

**Stage:** Advanced engineering/specialization · **Prerequisites:** 14, 31, systems foundations · **Estimate:** 35-60 hours

## Outcomes

Explain how computation, memory, communication, and recovery constrain large-scale training.

## Planned lessons

Accelerators; data/model/pipeline parallelism; collective communication; sharding; mixed precision; distributed optimization; checkpointing; throughput and utilization; failure recovery; reproducibility.

**Practice milestone:** estimate a small training workload and analyze scaling bottlenecks without requiring a cluster. **Status:** planned; large compute is not presumed available.
