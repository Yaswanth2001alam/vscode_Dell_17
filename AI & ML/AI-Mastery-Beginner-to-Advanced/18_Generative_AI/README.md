# 18 — Generative AI

**Stage:** Advanced · **Prerequisites:** 12, 16-17 · **Estimate:** 25-40 hours

## Outcomes

Compare major generative modeling approaches, understand training/inference objectives, and evaluate generated content responsibly.

## Planned lessons

Autoregressive generation; latent-variable models; GAN concepts; diffusion and score-based concepts; conditioning; sampling; quality/diversity evaluation; provenance, misuse, and limitations.

**Practice milestone:** reproduce a small generation experiment or analyze a controlled output set with explicit evaluation criteria. **Status:** planned; hardware and dependency requirements to be specified.
