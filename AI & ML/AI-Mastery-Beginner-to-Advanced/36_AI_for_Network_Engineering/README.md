# 36 — AI for Network Engineering

**Stage:** Expert specialization · **Prerequisites:** 06, 08-11, 21-23, 30-33 · **Estimate:** 35-60 hours

## Outcomes

Apply data and AI methods to network operations while respecting protocol semantics, operational risk, and human authority.

## Planned lessons

BGP, OSPF, and IS-IS troubleshooting; telemetry, packet loss, latency, and interface failures; anomaly detection and traffic forecasting; syslog classification; configuration validation; root-cause analysis; incident summarization; network-documentation RAG; read-only troubleshooting agents; Azure networking; observability and test-result analysis; change risk assessment.

## Labs and safety

Use synthetic telemetry, sanitized logs, and sample Cisco/Juniper/Arista/Azure concepts. Automation examples are read-only by default. Any potentially disruptive change must be clearly explained, reviewed, and explicitly approved by a human before execution.

**Practice milestone:** deliver a synthetic-data troubleshooting assistant that cites evidence, reports uncertainty, and cannot alter a device. **Status:** planned; no operational integrations are authorized or configured.
