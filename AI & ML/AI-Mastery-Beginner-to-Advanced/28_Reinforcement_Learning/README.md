# 28 — Reinforcement Learning

**Stage:** Advanced specialization · **Prerequisites:** 05, 08, 12 · **Estimate:** 30-50 hours

## Outcomes

Model sequential decisions and explain how reward, exploration, state, and policy affect learning and safety.

## Planned lessons

Markov decision processes; returns and discounting; Bellman equations; dynamic programming; Monte Carlo and temporal difference; Q-learning; policy gradients; exploration; evaluation and safe simulation.

**Practice milestone:** solve a small deterministic environment and compare with a simple baseline. **Status:** planned; no real-world control actions in practice labs.
