# 25 — Multimodal AI

**Stage:** Advanced specialization · **Prerequisites:** 13-17 · **Estimate:** 25-45 hours

## Outcomes

Reason about systems that jointly process or generate text, images, audio, video, and other signals.

## Planned lessons

Modality encoders; alignment and fusion; contrastive learning; vision-language models; audio/text systems; temporal/video representations; multimodal evaluation; privacy and modality-specific failure modes.

**Practice milestone:** evaluate one bounded multimodal task with a reproducible sample and modality-aware error analysis. **Status:** planned.
