# 27 — Speech and Audio AI

**Stage:** Advanced specialization · **Prerequisites:** 12-17 · **Estimate:** 25-45 hours

## Outcomes

Understand audio representations and build or evaluate a bounded speech/audio model task.

## Planned lessons

Sampling and waveforms; spectra and features; speech recognition; synthesis; speaker/audio classification; decoding; WER and other metrics; consent, privacy, and accessibility.

**Practice milestone:** evaluate an audio task using lawful, consented or synthetic data and analyze error types. **Status:** planned.
