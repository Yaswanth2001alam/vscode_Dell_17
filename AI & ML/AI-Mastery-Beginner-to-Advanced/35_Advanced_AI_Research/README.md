# 35 — Advanced AI Research

**Stage:** Expert specialization · **Prerequisites:** relevant advanced modules and mathematics · **Estimate:** 50-100+ hours

## Outcomes

Read, reproduce, critique, and communicate research with disciplined experiments and clear uncertainty.

## Planned lessons

Literature search; paper structure; hypotheses; baselines; dataset and metric scrutiny; experimental design; ablation; statistical uncertainty; reproducibility; peer critique; technical writing.

**Practice milestone:** reproduce a bounded result or conduct a careful negative/replication study with an auditable report. **Status:** planned; research mastery is an ongoing pursuit, not a timed promise.
